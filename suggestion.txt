There are legitimate ways AI adds value for this bike, just not through direct ECU flashing:
1. Diagnostics via K-Line — with a cheap K-Line to USB adapter (~50–100 DHS) and basic software, you can read live data (engine temp, TPS voltage, O2 voltage, RPM, solenoid duty cycle, DTCs). You could log this data and have AI analyze patterns — for example, detecting a clogging pilot jet from abnormal solenoid PWM drift, or predicting CVT wear from RPM-vs-speed curves. That's genuinely useful.
2. Predictive maintenance — feed your maintenance log data (oil change intervals, ambient temps, daily km) into an AI model and it could predict optimal service intervals for Marrakech conditions more precisely than the generic schedule.
3. Carburetor tuning guidance — AI can help you calculate optimal jet sizes for Marrakech altitude (~460m) and temperature range. The stock CVK jetting is for sea level European conditions — in Marrakech's thinner, hotter air, a slightly leaner pilot jet could improve efficiency.

Maintenance Schedule

Intervals shortened for Marrakech conditions: sustained heat (35-48 °C ambient), fine dust, and daily stop-and-go traffic.

Marrakech Factor Factory intervals assume moderate European climates. In Marrakech, oil degrades faster (heat), air filters clog sooner (dust), and CVT components wear faster (thermal expansion + fine grit). All intervals below are already adjusted.

Tires

Service Interval (Marrakech) Factory Interval Notes
Tire pressure check Weekly Monthly Front: 1.75 bar (25 PSI). Rear: 2.0 bar solo (29 PSI), 2.25 bar with passenger (32 PSI). Check cold. Marrakech temperature swings cause significant pressure fluctuation.
Tire condition inspection Weekly Monthly Check tread depth (≥1 mm), sidewall cracks, embedded debris, uneven wear.

Engine Oil — 10W-40

Service Interval (Marrakech) Factory Interval Notes
Oil change Every 1,500 km Every 3,000 km Heat accelerates oil breakdown. Semi-synthetic 10W-40 minimum. Check level weekly.
Oil filter / screen Every 3,000 km Every 6,000 km Clean mesh screen at every other oil change. Replace if torn or deformed.

Transmission Gear Oil — 80W-90

Service Interval (Marrakech) Factory Interval Notes
First change (break-in) At 1,000 km At 1,000 km Critical — removes metal shavings from new gears. Do not skip.
Regular change Every 10,000 km Every 10,000 km Sealed system, less affected by heat. Use GL-4 grade 80W-90. ~120-150 ml.

Air & Fuel System

Service Interval (Marrakech) Factory Interval Notes
Air filter — clean Every 3,000 km Every 4,000 km Marrakech dust is extremely fine. Tap out and blow with compressed air.
Air filter — replace Every 6,000 km Every 8,000 km Foam element degrades in heat. Replace if it crumbles when squeezed.
Carburetor pilot jet — clean Every 3,000 km As needed Preventive cleaning avoids idle issues. Use carb cleaner + compressed air through all jets.
Fuel filter Every 5,000 km Every 8,000 km Inline filter. Cheap insurance against jet clogging.
Spark plug Every 5,000 km Every 8,000 km Check gap and electrode color. Replace if deposits won't clean off. NGK CR7HSA or equivalent.

CVT & Drivetrain

Service Interval (Marrakech) Factory Interval Notes
CVT cover — inspect belt & rollers Every 5,000 km Every 8,000 km Look for cracks on belt, flat spots on rollers, dust accumulation in case.
Variator rollers — replace Every 10,000 km Every 15,000 km Highest-risk component. Replace proactively — roller failure can destroy the belt.
Drive belt — replace Every 12,000 km Every 18,000 km Measure width — replace if <17.5 mm (nominal ~18.5 mm). Heat accelerates rubber aging.
Centrifugal clutch — inspect Every 10,000 km Every 15,000 km Check spring tension and shoe lining thickness. Springs fatigue faster in heat.

Valve Train

Service Interval (Marrakech) Factory Interval Notes
Valve clearance — check & adjust Every 4,000 km Every 8,000 km IN: 0.08 mm, EX: 0.08 mm (cold). Both valves equal clearance. Valve guides wear faster in heat — listen for ticking.

Brakes

Service Interval (Marrakech) Factory Interval Notes
Brake pads — inspect Every 3,000 km Every 5,000 km Dust accelerates pad wear. Replace at ≤1 mm lining thickness.
Brake fluid — replace Every 12 months Every 2 years DOT 4. Heat causes faster moisture absorption. Boiling point drops = brake fade risk.
Brake caliper — clean & lube pistons Every 6,000 km As needed Dust + heat = piston seizure. Push back pistons, clean, apply silicone grease to seals.

Electrical

Service Interval (Marrakech) Factory Interval Notes
Battery — check Monthly Every 6 months Heat kills batteries. Check voltage (>12.4V at rest, >13V running). Top up if not sealed.
Starter motor brushes Every 15,000 km Every 20,000 km Inspect brush length. Replace if worn to wear indicator line. Symptoms: slow cranking.
Tracking Your Maintenance Record every service with date and odometer reading. A simple notebook or spreadsheet works — what matters is consistency. When paired with diagnostic data from the K-Line logger, you can predict optimal service timing instead of relying on fixed intervals.

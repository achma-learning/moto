Seasonal Maintenance Adjustments

Marrakech has two distinct seasons that affect the bike differently. Adjust your maintenance rhythm accordingly.

Summer (May – September) 35-48 °C

This is the hardest season on your engine. Air-cooled + 45°C ambient + Bab Doukkala traffic = peak thermal stress. Every maintenance interval matters more in summer.
AreaSummer AdjustmentWhy
Engine oil Check level twice weekly. Consider 1,000 km change intervals in July-August. Oil degrades ~2× faster above 110°C. Sustained idle in traffic pushes engine temps higher.
Tire pressure Always check cold (early morning). Expect +3-5 PSI rise when hot. Heat expands air significantly. Over-inflated hot tires lose grip on dusty roads.
Riding pattern Avoid sustained high-RPM rides during peak heat (12:00-16:00). Air-cooled engine has no thermostat — more RPM = more heat with less cooling airflow in traffic.
Cooling fins Clean weekly. Brush off all dust and debris. Dust insulates the fins, reducing cooling efficiency by up to 20%.
Battery Check monthly. If not sealed, check fluid level. Heat accelerates electrolyte evaporation. Battery life drops significantly above 40°C.
Air filter Check every 2,000 km instead of 3,000 km. Summer dust storms can clog a filter in days. A clogged filter runs the engine rich → carbon buildup.
Parking Park in shade whenever possible. UV degrades plastic body panels, rubber seals, and tire sidewalls. Fuel evaporates faster in direct sun.
CVT belt Monitor for glazing more closely. Heat accelerates rubber aging. Belt material softens and glazes faster in summer.

Winter (November – February) 5-15 °C mornings

AreaWinter AdjustmentWhy
Cold starts Let engine warm 30-60 seconds before riding. Use choke if equipped. Cold oil is thick and doesn't circulate well. Metal clearances are tighter when cold. Riding hard immediately causes accelerated wear.
Tire pressure Check more frequently — pressure drops ~1 PSI per 5°C temperature drop. Morning temps of 5°C can drop tire pressure below safe levels if only checked in warmer weather.
Fuel Keep tank full to minimize air space. Use fuel within 2-3 weeks. Temperature swings cause condensation inside the tank. Water in fuel clogs the pilot jet and corrodes the float bowl.
Brakes Expect different feel in cold. Apply brakes lightly for first few stops. Cold brake pads need 3-4 applications to reach operating temperature. Stopping distance is longer.
Battery Monitor voltage more closely. Charge if below 12.2V. Battery capacity drops 20-30% in cold. A marginal battery that works in summer will fail in winter.
Rain After riding in rain, dry brakes by applying them lightly several times. Wet disc brakes have dramatically reduced stopping power. Rust can form on the disc overnight.

Transition Periods (March-April, September-October)

Best Time for Major Services Moderate temperatures (20-30°C) make these the ideal months for major maintenance: CVT belt/roller replacement, brake overhaul, valve clearance check, thorough cleaning. Plan your big services here.
ServiceWhy Now
CVT belt & roller inspection/replacementBefore summer heat degrades new parts. After winter ensures nothing wore during cold starts.
Brake system overhaulClean calipers, replace fluid, inspect pads — prepare for summer heat cycling.
Valve clearance checkComfortable garage temperature. Catches any winter drift before summer heat makes it worse.
Full fluid changeFresh oil going into summer. Fresh brake fluid with maximum boiling point.
Air filter replacementNew filter going into the dustiest season (summer).
Throttle & brake cable lubricationPrevents cable snap during hot-weather riding when cables expand.

Extended Storage / Low-Use Periods

During Ramadan, holidays, or any period the bike sits for >2 weeks:

  • Drain the carburetor float bowl — Turn the float bowl drain screw and let fuel drain into a rag. Stale fuel varnishes the pilot jet within 2-3 weeks in Marrakech heat.
  • Fill the fuel tank — A full tank has less air space for condensation to form.
  • Connect a battery maintainer — Or disconnect the battery negative terminal. A parked bike's battery slowly discharges from the ECU and clock circuits.
  • Cover the bike — Protect from dust, UV, and rain. A breathable cover prevents trapped moisture.
  • Before restarting — Check oil level, tire pressure, brake feel, and battery voltage. Run the engine for 2-3 minutes before riding.

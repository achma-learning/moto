Engine Oil Brand Guide

For the Kymco Agility 50 4T, use a 4T motorcycle oil (not car oil). Choose viscosity based on season and engine condition.

Viscosity Quick Reference
  • 10W-40 — Year-round standard, mountain zone winter/spring
  • 15W-40 / 10W-50 / 15W-50 — Marrakech summer (40 °C+)
  • 10W-50 / 15W-50 — Extreme summer heat only (July–August)
  • 20W-50 — Older / high-mileage engine (worn clearances)

AliExpress

Best for generic consumables (filters, rollers, belts) and electronics (ESP32, sensors). 2-4 week shipping. Verify dimensions before ordering.

Jumia.ma

Some parts available domestically. Good for tools, oils, and basic consumables. Faster delivery than AliExpress. Check seller ratings.

Local Sourcing (Marrakech)

Moto Parts Shops

Concentrated in the Bab Doukkala and Sidi Youssef Ben Ali areas. Cheapest option for common consumables (oil, spark plugs, brake pads, filters). Tips:

  • Bring the old part with you for exact matching
  • Prices are negotiable — always ask for the price first, then negotiate
  • Check packaging for genuine NGK/brand labels vs unmarked generics
  • Oil: buy sealed bottles from Marjane or Total stations to ensure authenticity

Marjane / Carrefour

Reliable for branded oils (Castrol, Motul, Total), basic tools, brake fluid, and general supplies. Not specialized for scooter parts, but consistent quality for fluids.

Total / Shell Stations

Some carry motorcycle-specific oil (10W-40 JASO MA). More expensive than Marjane but convenient and guaranteed genuine.

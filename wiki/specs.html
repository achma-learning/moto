Diagnostic Connector (K-Line)

ECU Diagnostic Protocol ISO 14230 / KWP2000 via K-Line. Recommended hardware: ESP32 + L9637D transceiver (~150 DHS total). Cheap ELM327 V2.1 clones (common in Morocco) are unreliable on K-Line/ISO 14230 — they only handle CAN protocol reliably. Only genuine ELM327 V1.5+ adapters work, and those are rare locally. See Diagnostics page for the full hardware guide.

Riding & Engine Impact

Riding PatternPredominantly stop-and-go urban traffic with frequent medina-adjacent congestion
Thermal Stress NoteAt 460 m, air density is ~5 % lower than sea level → slightly reduced cooling on air-cooled engines
Jetting NoteStock jetting is for sea-level ~15 °C Europe. At 460 m / 40 °C+, air is substantially thinner — one-step leaner pilot jet may improve idle stability. Re-evaluate main jet if sustained full-throttle >3,000 RPM.
Worst-Case Thermal Scenario 48 °C ambient day, stuck in Bab Doukkala traffic at idle, with Saharan dust clogging the air filter. The 47.6 °C peak recorded in July 2024 is no longer an outlier — it's becoming the new ceiling your air-cooled cylinder must survive every summer. Design your maintenance schedule around this scenario.
2024 Heatwave Trend July and August 2024 saw prolonged consecutive heatwaves with increasing frequency and intensity. Combined with the 7th consecutive drought year, dust levels are rising and engine cooling is under more stress than ever. Oil change intervals and air filter service are more critical now than when this bike was designed.

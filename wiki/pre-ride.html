Pre-Ride Checklist

Run through this before every ride. Print this page for a garage-friendly reference (Ctrl+P).

Print-Friendly This page is designed to fit on one A4 sheet when printed. Use Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac) to print a copy for your garage.

Every Ride Critical

  • Engine oil level — Dipstick check, bike upright on center stand. Between the two marks.
  • Tire pressure (visual) — Tires should look properly inflated, not flat or bulging. Gauge check weekly.
  • Front brake lever — Firm feel, no sponge. Should engage within first 1/3 of lever travel.
  • Rear brake — 10-20 mm free play at pedal before engagement.
  • Lights & signals — Headlight (low/high), tail light, brake light (both levers), turn signals.
  • Fuel level — Check gauge or open cap. Don't ride on fumes — sediment gets sucked into carb.
  • Throttle free play — 2-6 mm of slack before cable engages. Throttle should snap closed when released.
  • Mirrors — Adjusted and tight. Vibration loosens them over time.
  • Center stand / side stand — Retracts fully and stays up while riding.

Weekly Checks

  • Tire pressure with gauge — Front: 1.75 bar (25 PSI). Rear: 2.0 bar solo / 2.25 bar with passenger. Check cold.
  • Tire condition — Tread depth ≥1 mm, no sidewall cracks, no embedded debris.
  • Cooling fins — Clean and unblocked. Brush off dust and debris from cylinder fins.
  • Battery voltage — >12.4V at rest (multimeter on battery terminals, engine off).
  • Under-seat area — Check for water ingress, loose items, or rodent damage to wiring.
  • Chain/drive inspection — Listen for unusual noises from CVT area on short test ride.

Marrakech-Specific

  • Summer (May-Sep): Check cooling fin blockage more frequently — dust and debris accumulate fast in 40°C+ heat.
  • After dust storms: Inspect air filter immediately. Heavy dust events can clog the filter in a single day.
  • Bike sat >2 weeks: Drain carburetor float bowl before restart (float bowl drain screw at bottom of carb). Stale fuel varnishes the pilot jet.
  • Rainy season (Oct-Mar): Check brake performance after rain — disc brakes need a few pumps to dry. Inspect for rust on disc surface.
If Anything Feels Wrong, Don't Ride Spongy brakes, unusual engine noise, steering wobble, or fluid leaks all warrant investigation before riding. A 10-minute check prevents roadside breakdowns in Marrakech traffic.

Kymco Agility 50 4T

Durability-focused maintenance wiki for Marrakech conditions — heat, dust, and stop-and-go traffic.

Core Philosophy: Durability Over Performance All decisions — oil spec, maintenance intervals, hardware choices — are optimized for longevity under Marrakech conditions (40 °C+ summers, dust, heavy traffic), not speed or power.

Quick Reference

Engine 49.5 cc, 4-stroke, SOHC, 2-valve, air-cooled
Fuel System Keihin CVK carburetor
ECU Dellorto LDC8 (16-bit)
Transmission CVT (belt + variator) + centrifugal clutch
Engine Oil 10W-40 • ~750-800 ml • every 1,500 km
Gear Oil 80W-90 • ~120-150 ml • every 10,000 km
Diagnostic Protocol ISO 14230 / KWP2000 via K-Line
Climate Marrakech — hot, dusty, stop-and-go

Top Warnings

CVT Variator Rollers — Highest Failure Risk Worn rollers cause poor acceleration, RPM hunting, and eventual belt damage. Inspect every 5,000 km in Marrakech heat. Replace at first sign of flat-spotting.
Carburetor Pilot Jet Clogging Marrakech dust + fuel sediment = frequent pilot jet clogging. Symptoms: rough idle, stalling at stops, high idle solenoid PWM (>90%). Clean every 3,000 km or at first symptom.
Two Oil Systems — Never Mix Engine oil (10W-40) and transmission gear oil (80W-90) are completely separate. Different drain plugs, different capacities, different intervals. Learn the difference →
